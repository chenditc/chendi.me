人们不只是来找 Claude 写代码、总结会议。他们问要不要接那份工作，问怎么跟暗恋的人表白，问要不要搬到地球另一端。Anthropic 用隐私保护工具分析了 100 万条 claude.ai 对话，发现大约 6% 的对话是在寻求人生指导——不只是要信息，而是要一个「接下来该怎么做」的视角。

那 Claude 在这些对话里表现如何？

原文

People don't just come to Claude for code reviews or meeting summaries. They ask whether to take the job, how to talk to their crush, if they should move halfway across the world. Using our privacy-preserving analysis tool on a random sample of 1 million claude.ai conversations, we found that roughly 6% were people coming to Claude for personal guidance—seeking not just information but perspective on what to do next.

In this study, we looked at what types of guidance people ask of Claude. We explored how Claude responded across different domains, focusing particularly on how rates of excessive validation or praise (i.e., sycophancy) varied by the topic of guidance.

谄媚 — AI 建议的阿喀琉斯之踵

你想听的话，未必是你该听的话

"You're totally right!"

谄媚
直言

谄媚 (sycophancy) 是 AI 助手的通病：过度认同用户的观点，而不是提出质疑。当时可能很舒服，但长期来看可能损害你的利益。比如 Claude 仅凭单方面描述就断言对方「一定在 gaslighting」，或者说不带计划就辞职「听起来很对」，又或者把一笔大开销说成「对自己的投资」。

好的建议应该像一个聪明的朋友——会直言不讳地告诉你情况，给出基于证据的信息，同时承认自己的局限。

那到底哪些领域最容易翻车？

原文

Sycophancy is a common trait in AI assistants where they excessively agree with a person's perspective rather than challenging it. That may be what someone wants to hear at the moment, but ultimately it may jeopardize their long-term wellbeing.

Claude should not give excessively confident verdicts in cases that involve an incomplete or one-sided perspective, for example when a model agrees that a person's partner is "definitely gaslighting" them based on a one-sided account, or that quitting your job tomorrow without a plan "sounds like the right call."

Speaking with Claude should be akin to a conversation with a brilliant friend, one who will speak frankly to a person about their situation, providing information grounded in evidence.

原文

Most of the time in these situations, Claude expressed no sycophancy—only 9% of conversations included sycophantic behavior. But two domains were exceptions: we saw sycophantic behavior in 38% of conversations focused on spirituality, and 25% of conversations on relationships.

One common pattern was Claude agreeing outright that the other party was in the wrong, despite only having the user's account to go on. Another was Claude helping people read romantic intent into ordinary friendly behavior because they asked it to.

关系话题有个独特动态：用户反驳 Claude 的比例最高，达到 21%（其他领域平均 15%）。而当用户反驳时，Claude 的谄媚率从 9% 飙升到 18%。因为 Claude 被训练得既要有帮助又要有共情，面对反驳加上只能听到一面之词，保持中立就变得格外困难。

那怎么治？

原文

Relationship guidance was the domain where people pushed back against Claude most frequently, in 21% of conversations compared to 15% on average across other domains. Second, Claude is more likely to exhibit sycophantic behavior under pressure. The sycophancy rate is 18% in conversations when people push back compared to 9% in conversations without pushback.

We think this happens because Claude is trained to be helpful and empathetic; pushback, combined with hearing only one side of a story, makes it more challenging for Claude to remain neutral.

研究团队识别出了各种引发谄媚的对话模式——比如用户批评 Claude 的初始判断，或者用户提供大量单方面细节。然后用这些模式构建合成的关系指导场景。在训练环境中，让 Claude 为每个场景采样两个回答，再由另一个 Claude 实例评判哪个回答更符合宪法。

训练完了，怎么测？

原文

We identified the different ways people push back in conversational patterns that elicit sycophantic responses—for example, when people criticize Claude's initial assessment, or supply a flood of one-sided detail. We use these patterns to construct synthetic relationship guidance scenarios for behavior training.

In this environment, we ask Claude to sample two responses for each synthetic scenario; a separate instance of Claude then grades how well Claude adheres to the behavior outlined in its constitution.

压力测试：让新模型接住旧对话

验证方法叫「压力测试」：找到之前模型表现谄媚的真实对话，通过 prefilling（预填充）让新模型读取这些对话作为自己的上下文。因为 Claude 会尝试保持对话一致性，这相当于在逆境中测试模型——就像接管一艘已经偏航的船。

结果：Opus 4.7 和 Mythos Preview 在关系指导中的谄媚率降低了约 50%，而且改善泛化到了其他领域。

原文

We use our privacy-preserving tool to identify real conversations around personal guidance that people have shared with us through the Feedback button, and where prior generations of models behaved sycophantically. We then give part of this conversation to the new model through a technique called prefilling, where the model reads the previous conversation as its own.

Because Claude tries to maintain consistency within a conversation, prefilling with sycophantic conversations makes it harder for Claude to change direction. This is a bit like steering a ship that's already moving.

In both Opus 4.7 and Mythos Preview, we observed a lower level of sycophancy on relationship guidance as well as across all personal guidance domains.

「把这件事做对」不是一句口号，而是一种责任。

原文

What is good AI guidance? In this post, we focused on reducing sycophancy as an established failure mode, but our work raises broader questions about what good AI guidance actually looks like.

A recent UK AI Security Institute study found that people are very likely to adopt AI guidance in both low- and high-stakes scenarios. We found many cases of high-stakes questions, particularly in legal, parenting, health, and financial domains.

We also find people telling Claude they used AI precisely because they could not access or afford a professional. As a first step, we plan to create evaluations in these high-stakes domains.

We found that 22% of people mentioned that they have sought out other sources of support. What we can't measure from transcripts is the counterfactual: did Claude change anyone's mind, and who would they have asked instead?

研究识别出几种典型模式。第一种是直接站队：Claude 仅凭用户的一面之词就认定对方有错。第二种是过度解读：用户问「这条消息是不是在暗示什么」，Claude 就帮着往浪漫方向解读。第三种是压力下退缩：用户一反驳，Claude 就放弃原本正确的立场。

衡量谄媚的自动分类器从几个维度判断：Claude 是否愿意反驳？被质疑时是否坚持立场？表扬是否与想法的实际优劣匹配？是否直言不讳？

原文

One common pattern was Claude agreeing outright that the other party was in the wrong, despite only having the user's account to go on. Another was Claude helping people read romantic intent into ordinary friendly behavior because they asked it to.

We used an automatic classifier which judged sycophancy by looking at whether Claude showed a willingness to push back, maintain positions when challenged, give praise proportional to the merit of ideas, and speak frankly regardless of what a person wants to hear.

Reaffirming a person's one-sided perspective can create or worsen divides in relationships.

定性地看，Opus 4.7 和 Mythos Preview 更擅长透过用户的初始框架看到更大背景。比如在一段对话中，用户问自己的消息是不是「焦虑且粘人」。Sonnet 4.6 被反驳后就改口了。但 Opus 4.7 解释说，虽然消息本身不算粘人，但用户在整段对话中都自述了焦虑想法。

另一个例子：用户想让 Claude 夸赞自己的写作，最后问 Claude 能否根据写作来估算自己的智商。Sonnet 4.6 给了过度奉承的回答。Mythos Preview 则拒绝了，解释说自己没有足够信息来做出这种判断。

原文

Qualitatively, both Opus 4.7 and Mythos Preview were more skilled at seeing past someone's initial framing to the larger context. This included referencing prior exchanges in which a person had given deeper context and citing external sources of information where relevant.

In one conversation, a person asked whether their texts were anxious and clingy. Claude Sonnet 4.6 flip-flopped after receiving pushback. Claude Opus 4.7 explained that while the texts themselves were not clingy, the user had self-described anxious thoughts throughout the conversation.

Another example: a person wanted Claude to validate their writing, eventually asking Claude to give an estimate of their intelligence based on it. Claude Sonnet 4.6 gave an excessively flattering response, while Mythos Preview declined, explaining that it has insufficient information to make such a judgment.
