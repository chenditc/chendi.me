30 亿参数的专业模型
击败了所有商用前沿 API

这篇来自 Dharma-AI 的文章开场就抛出了一个反直觉的事实：在一个精心测量的企业级任务上，一个只有 30 亿参数的专业化模型在质量、成本和稳定性三个维度上全面超越了所有被测试的商用前沿 API——包括 Claude Opus 4.6、Gemini 3.1 Pro 和 GPT-5.4。具体来说，这个专业化 3B 模型在巴西葡萄牙语 OCR 基准测试上拿到了 0.911 的综合分数，而排名第二的 Claude Opus 4.6 只有 0.833——两者之间的 8 个百分点差距比排行榜上任何其他相邻选手之间的差距都大。在成本维度上，差距更加悬殊：专业化 3B 模型的推理成本大约是 Claude Opus 4.6 的 五十二分之一。在生产稳定性上，该模型的文本退化率（text degeneration rate）仅为 0.20%，是所有被评估模型中最低的。质量第一、成本最低、稳定性最好——全部由同一个最小的专业化模型领跑。

When a model's training history is moved close enough to its deployment task, parameter count stops being the decisive variable. A 3-billion-parameter specialized model outperformed every commercial frontier API tested in a well-measured enterprise domain — at roughly fifty times lower cost.

On extraction quality, the highest-scoring model in the comparison was the specialized 3-billion-parameter model. It scored 0.911 on the benchmark's composite score, which combines edit-distance similarity with n-gram overlap.

The specialized model finished first, and the gap to Claude Opus 4.6 — close to eight percentage points — was wider than any other gap between adjacent finishers in the comparison.

On cost, the gap was far wider. The specialized 3B model ran at approximately fifty-two times lower cost per million pages than Claude Opus 4.6.

On production stability, the same model produced the lowest text-degeneration rate evaluated. The 3B model recorded 0.20% on this benchmark.

采购默认值：
「选最大的」曾经是对的

这个默认值不是偶然形成的。过去三年里，它确实是正确的。当 GPT-4 发布时，它在所有重要基准上都超越了每一个更小的模型。这个模式在 Claude 3、Gemini 1.5 以及 2025 年的每一代前沿模型发布中不断重复。OpenAI 早在 2020 年就通过 Kaplan et al. 的 scaling laws 把这个经验关系形式化了：能力随参数数量和训练计算量而扩展。因此得出的教训看起来很简单——选最大模型的买家，平均而言就是选了性能最好的工具。在缺乏更精细信号的情况下，默认选择规模是理性的做法。问题在于，这个假设之所以成立，是因为它所依据的对比集并不完整。对比集里缺少的不是更小的前沿模型，而是一种完全不同类型的模型——专业化模型：一个训练历史被刻意向部署任务靠拢的模型，通过一系列 fine-tuning 步骤把一个较小的基座模型适配到它将要部署的领域。

The procurement default did not arrive by accident. It arrived because, for most of the past three years, it was correct.

When GPT-4 was released, it outperformed every smaller model on the benchmarks that mattered. The pattern repeated, with refinements, through Claude 3, Gemini 1.5, and each generation of frontier release in 2025. Capability scaled with parameter count and with training compute (Kaplan et al., 2020) — the empirical relationship OpenAI's scaling laws had formalized years earlier.

The assumption was defensible because, for most of the comparisons that produced it, it was correct. What changed was not that the assumption had always been wrong. What changed was that the comparison set on which it rested may not have been complete.

What was missing was a different kind of model. Not a smaller frontier model. A specialized model — one whose training history had been deliberately moved closer to the task it would be asked to do, through a sequence of fine-tuning steps that adapted a smaller base to the domain it would be deployed in.

决定性变量：
分布对齐 > 参数规模

30 亿参数的模型专注于部署任务，所以经常能超越参数分散在大量任务无关材料上的大模型——这部分直觉上容易理解。但这篇论文的发现更进一步：关键变量不仅是参数如何分配，而是模型的训练历史被多大程度地向任务靠拢。在报告的实验中，这个变量对相对性能的预测力超过了其他所有被测试的因素——包括参数数量。论文原文直接点明了这一点：「contextual specialization can be more decisive than number of model parameters alone」。决定一个模型是否表现最好的因素，不是参数数量，而是它的训练轨迹与部署任务之间的距离有多近。这意味着采购框架中优先级的反转——在传统默认值下，参数数量是主导变量，训练历史是次要修正；而在这篇论文提出的框架下，对任务的分布对齐成为主导变量，参数数量退为影响对齐收益大小的若干因素之一。论文还用数据直接证明了这一点：同样是 3B 架构，经过同样的训练流程，已经为通用 OCR 预训练过的 Nanonets-OCR2 达到了 0.921 / 退化率 0.20%，而通用模型 Qwen2.5-VL-3B 只达到 0.793 / 退化率 1.41%。同架构、同训练——变量是模型在流程开始前就已经朝任务走了多远。

A 3-billion-parameter model focused on the deployment task will often outperform a much larger model whose parameters are spread across material the task will never touch — other languages, other corpora, other domains. What the paper adds goes further: one of the important variables is not only how parameters are allocated, but how the model's training history has been moved toward the task.

The paper names this directly. In its discussion, the authors describe the result as supporting the claim that "contextual specialization can be more decisive than number of model parameters alone." What determined whether a model performed best was not parameter count, but how close its training trajectory had been moved to its deployment task.

Specialization is not a way to compensate for being small. It is a way to be aligned.

The numbers bear the framing out. The 3B Nanonets-OCR2 — already specialized for general OCR before the paper began — was fine-tuned on the target domain through supervised fine-tuning and Direct Preference Optimization, and reached 0.921 with a 0.20% degeneration rate. A 3B general-purpose model of identical architecture, Qwen2.5-VL-3B, was run through the same procedure and reached 0.793 with 1.41% degeneration. Same architecture, same training, different result. The variable was the distance the model had already traveled toward the task before the procedure began.

专业化的复利效应：
起点不同，终点天差地别

分布对齐不是一个模型「有或没有」的二元状态——它是一个可以逐级提升的层级位置。通用模型坐在底层；通用领域专家（为更广泛的工作类别训练过）坐在上面；特定领域专家（为具体部署任务训练过）坐在最上面。关键发现是：同样的下游训练，在不同起点上产生完全不同的结果。论文用两组对照实验结构性地证明了这一点。在 7B 参数规模：从通用起点 Qwen2.5-VL-7B 出发达到 0.906 / 退化率 1.01%，但从已经为通用 OCR 专业化过的 olmOCR-2-7B 出发达到 0.927 / 退化率仅 0.40%——质量提升 2.3%，退化率减半。在 3B 参数规模更加戏剧性：通用起点 0.793 / 1.41% vs 专业起点 0.921 / 0.20%——质量跃升 16 个百分点，退化率降低了 7 倍。两组对照、两个参数规模、一致的结论：专业化是累积的。一个已经向目标任务的更广泛类别靠拢的模型，从同样的领域特定训练中获益更多。

Alignment is not a single thing a model either has or lacks. It is a position on a hierarchy that can be moved up one step at a time.

At the 7-billion-parameter scale: the best fine-tuned model derived from Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct — a general-purpose start — reached 0.906 with a 1.01% degeneration rate. The same training applied to olmOCR-2–7B — already specialized for general OCR — reached 0.927 with 0.40% degeneration. The quality gain was approximately 2.3 percent; the degeneration rate fell by nearly half.

At the 3-billion-parameter scale: Qwen2.5-VL-3B finished at 0.793 with 1.41% degeneration; Nanonets-OCR2–3B finished at 0.921 with 0.20% degeneration. Same procedure, same architecture class, different starting position. The quality gain was approximately 16 percent; the degeneration rate fell by a factor of roughly seven.

Two pairs, two parameter scales, two consistent results. Specialization accumulates. A model already moved closer to the broader category of its eventual task benefits more from the same domain-specific training than a model starting from a wider distribution.

三个战略问题：
采购决策应该怎么变？

这篇论文最实用的价值不是告诉企业下一步该做什么，而是提供了三个应该被追问的问题。第一个问题：分布对齐是否应该与参数数量一起被提升为 AI 评估中的一级变量？论文的证据并不主张把它放在参数数量之上，而是更审慎地论证——对齐作为变量的影响足够大，应该被显式测试而不是假设它可以忽略。第二个问题：公共基准测试的领先，本身是否足以作为企业采购决策的依据？在这个精心测量的领域里，领跑公共基准的模型并不是交付最佳结果的模型。如果这种偏差在其他领域也出现，企业评估可能需要额外一层证据——在代表实际部署场景的工作负载上运行测试。第三个问题是关于架构而非方法的：如果对齐是一个会复利累积的层级位置，那么起始模型的选择——不仅仅是 fine-tuning 流程——本身就成为一个战略决策。更深层的含义可能是组织层面的：企业最终可能从建立一个逐步对齐到自身领域和工作流的模型生态系统中获益更多，而不是去寻找一个万能的通用模型。

The first: whether distributional alignment should be elevated alongside parameter count as a first-class variable in serious AI evaluation. The paper's evidence does not argue for elevating it above parameter count. It argues, more modestly, that alignment is large enough as a variable to be tested explicitly rather than assumed to be small.

The second follows: is benchmark leadership, on its own, sufficient evidence for an enterprise procurement decision? In one well-measured domain, the model that led the public benchmarks was not the model that delivered the best result.

The third is about architecture, not method. If alignment is a position on a hierarchy that compounds, the choice of starting model — not only the fine-tuning procedure — becomes a strategic decision in its own right. A starting model already closer to the deployment task may produce materially better outcomes than a larger, more general model under the same training budget. But the deeper implication may be organizational rather than procedural. If specialization compounds, enterprises may eventually benefit less from searching for a single universally capable model than from building an ecosystem of models progressively aligned to their own domains, workflows, and operational constraints.

有边界的重构：
不是规模不重要，而是少了一个变量

这篇文章的贡献是故意狭窄的。它没有论证前沿模型是劣等的、是可弃用的、或者采购默认值应该被颠倒。它的论点是：基于一篇论文的证据，前沿模型不一定是每个企业 AI 工作负载的最佳选择。在所报告的实验中，训练历史与部署任务更紧密对齐的小型专业化模型，在质量、成本和生产稳定性上都超越了被评估的大型商用 API。因此其含义不是前沿模型不好，而是专业化历史作为一个变量，对企业 AI 系统的战略重要性可能远超当前大多数评估框架的假设。作者写这篇文章的目的不是论证规模不再重要，而是要隔离出一个当前企业 AI 对话中可能仍然被低估的变量。训练历史是可以被观察、评估并通过连续的专业化阶段向部署任务靠拢的。在论文报告的对比中，这个关系实质性地改变了每个被评估模型的排名。它是否也会在其他地方改变排名——这是下一组实验要回答的问题。

The article's contribution is narrow, by design. It has not argued that frontier models are inferior, or disposable, or that the procurement default should be inverted. It has argued, on the strength of one paper's evidence, that frontier models are not necessarily the best-performing choice for every enterprise AI workload.

In the experiments reported, smaller specialized models with training histories more closely aligned to the deployment task achieved superior quality, lower cost, and greater production stability than the larger commercial APIs evaluated. The implication is not that frontier models are inferior. It is that specialization history may be a more strategically important variable for enterprise AI systems than many evaluation frameworks currently assume.

We wrote this article not to argue that scale no longer matters, but to isolate a variable the current enterprise AI conversation may still underweight. Training history can be observed, evaluated, and moved closer to a deployment task through successive stages of specialization. In the comparisons reported in the paper, that relationship materially changed the ranking of every model evaluated. Whether it changes rankings elsewhere is a question for the next set of experiments.

The benchmark used in the paper was a domain-specific evaluation: Brazilian Portuguese OCR across printed documents, handwritten text, and legal and administrative records.

Specialized 3B Model: 0.911. Claude Opus 4.6: 0.833. Gemini 3.1 Pro: 0.820. GPT-5.4: 0.750. Google Vision: 0.686. Google Document AI: 0.640. GPT-4o: 0.635. Amazon Textract: 0.618. Mistral OCR 3: 0.574.

These three findings — quality, cost, and stability, all led by the same 3B specialized model — are the article's empirical anchor. Together, they make the empirical case stronger than any single finding would alone.

复利证据：
同训练 + 不同起点 = 天壤之别

论文的证据是结构性的：两组对照实验，两个参数规模，直接证明了专业化如何累积。在 7B 参数规模：从通用模型 Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct 出发的最佳微调版本达到了 0.906 / 退化率 1.01%；而从已经为通用 OCR 专业化过的 olmOCR-2-7B 出发，经过同样的训练，达到了 0.927 / 退化率仅 0.40%。质量提升约 2.3 个百分点，退化率降低了将近一半。同样的架构、同样的数据、同样的训练流水线——唯一的变量是起始位置。在 3B 参数规模，差距更加戏剧性：Qwen2.5-VL-3B 以 0.793 / 退化率 1.41% 完成训练，而 Nanonets-OCR2-3B 以 0.921 / 退化率 0.20% 完成——同样的流程、同样的架构类别、不同的起始位置。质量跃升约 16 个百分点，退化率降低了约 7 倍。论文将此描述为三个专业化层级：Level 1 是通用基座（vanilla generalist），Level 2 是通用领域 OCR 专家（general-domain OCR specialist），Level 3 是特定领域 OCR 专家（domain-specific OCR specialist），以及一个投射的 Level N 代表未来更细粒度的子领域专业化。每个层级都建立在前一个层级编码的分布之上——多个训练阶段可以逐步将模型向目标任务分布靠拢，即使在相似的架构和计算约束下也能产生实质性不同的结果。

At the 7-billion-parameter scale: the best fine-tuned model derived from Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct — a general-purpose start — reached 0.906 with a 1.01% degeneration rate. The same training applied to olmOCR-2–7B — already specialized for general OCR — reached 0.927 with 0.40% degeneration.

There are levels of specialization, and each level builds on the distribution encoded by the one before it. Multiple stages of training can progressively move a model closer to the target task distribution, producing materially different downstream outcomes even under similar architectural and computational constraints.

That pattern — alignment as an accumulating quantity — is the article's strongest claim from the paper's evidence.
